# 📌 About the Project

LifeLink is a Blood and Organ Donation Coordination System created to solve a major problem during medical emergencies: **delay in finding, coordinating, and transporting blood or organs.**

The platform connects:

- 🏥 Hospitals
- 🩸 Blood Banks
- ❤️ Blood Donors
- 🫀 Organ Donation Networks
- 🚑 Emergency Services
- 🚁 Medical Logistics

LifeLink does **not sell blood or organs**.

The donation remains free, voluntary, legal, and ethical.

Our platform provides the **technology, coordination, matching, and logistics services** around the donation process.

---

# 🚨 Problem Statement

During emergency situations, hospitals may face:

- Lack of required blood groups
- Difficulty finding suitable donors
- Manual phone-call coordination
- Verification delays
- Transportation problems
- Lack of real-time information
- Difficulty coordinating between hospitals and blood banks

Even when blood is available nearby, delays in communication and transportation can become critical.

### 🎯 Our Goal

> **Reduce the time between an emergency requirement and the availability of the required blood or medical resource.**

---

# 💡 Our Solution

LifeLink works as a **hospital-triggered emergency coordination system**.

The patient does not need to search for donors during a panic situation.

### Emergency Flow

```text
Patient Emergency
       ↓
Doctor / Hospital Creates Request
       ↓
LifeLink Emergency System
       ↓
Find Suitable Donor / Blood Source
       ↓
Verify Availability
       ↓
Send Emergency Alert
       ↓
Arrange Transportation
       ↓
Hospital Receives Blood / Medical Resource
